I just flipped the switch. [Tradr](https://www.tradr.cloud) is live and taking sign-ups.

::lead[Go make an account at app.tradr.cloud. It's free.]

_(that's [app.tradr.cloud/register](https://app.tradr.cloud/register), and the pillows are fluffed, mostly.)_

Is it a v1.0? Still no.\
Is it live? Yes.

Tradr is an open-source stock & options trading journal. The build you'll land on today is v0.11.3. The journal part is finished and I've traded on it every day for months. The edges are rough in places, and I'd rather you find them than sit here guessing at them.

## What you get

A free account (I'm calling it Hosted Free) gets you 1 brokerage account, 1,000 positions, 6 months of analytics, and 10 CSV imports a month. For one broker and a normal number of trades, that's plenty. If you outgrow it, Hosted Pro takes the caps off for $10 a month. That's a launch price; it goes to $15 later.

The journal does the things I could never get a spreadsheet to do reliably. Positions with many fills, scaled in and scaled out, with the averages and the fee-accurate realised P&L coming out right. A double-entry ledger underneath so the cash reconciles. Greeks and OCC symbol parsing for the options crowd. A sizing calculator so you know your risk before you're in the trade. CSV import from whatever broker you use. And a guided tour with demo data, so you can poke around a populated Tradr before you enter a single real fill.

It never places an order. Nothing in it touches your broker.

## What's missing, on purpose

The AI advisor is switched off on the hosted app. It exists, it runs on your own Claude or OpenAI key, and you can use it today if you self-host. I turned it off on hosted on Monday because it needs real work before it's good enough, and shipping it half-baked on launch day felt worse than shipping without it. The Unusual Whales options-chain lookup sits behind the same switch, so that one is self-hosted only for now too.

There's no direct broker sync yet, CSV only. And the API will move around until 1.0.

## If you'd rather run it yourself

Tradr is Apache 2.0 and built to self-host. One Docker Compose file gets you the whole thing, advisor included, and nothing phones home unless you turn it on. [Here's the how-to](https://docs.tradr.cloud/self-hosting/docker-compose/), and the [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/madmatt112/tradr).

## What I'm watching

Seven months after the layoff, this is the first thing I've built that gets a public verdict. The number I care about is how many people log a real trade in their first 30 days. Sign-ups are cheap.

So: sign up, load the demo data if you want to look around, then clear it and book one real trade. If something breaks or confuses you, [open an issue](https://github.com/madmatt112/tradr/issues), or reply to this email if you're reading it in your inbox.
