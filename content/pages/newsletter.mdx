I lost my full-time job as an infrastructure engineer in January 2026. Since then I’ve been building software, helping local non-profits and small businesses untangle their technology, and day-trading options. (Besides doing shadow & soul work with my therapist, raising my kids, and going to [Pilates classes](https://www.mobilefitnessandpilates.com/).) There’s no deadline on any of it. Good things come when they come, and all I can do is keep putting in the work.

Field Notes is the story of how I’m doing it, and what I learn along the way. You’ll see what works and what doesn’t. I’ll share the tools I use, the systems I build, and the people I meet. Hopefully you’ll also see how I make it all into a sustainable business that supports my family and my community.
