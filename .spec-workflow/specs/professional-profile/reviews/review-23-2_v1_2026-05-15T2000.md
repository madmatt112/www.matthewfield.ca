### INFO: Scroll-jitter sample window may close after smooth-scroll completes on slow runs
- **File:** /home/mcf/repo/matthew-field.ca/e2e/tests/contact-reduced-motion.test.ts:63
- **Issue:** The test samples `getBoundingClientRect().top` twice with a 50ms gap after `expect(successHeading).toBeVisible()` resolves. A forced `behavior:'smooth'` (regression case) typically animates over ~200-400ms, so on a fast machine the window is likely to overlap with an in-flight smooth scroll and fail correctly. On a slow CI worker, however, the round-trip between visibility-await and the first `evaluate` could exceed the smooth-scroll duration, meaning both samples land at the post-scroll resting position and the regression slips through. Consider either (a) starting the sample window synchronously right after the click and before awaiting visibility, or (b) widening the inter-sample gap to ~400ms to bracket a typical smooth-scroll. The current test still satisfies the spec's third success criterion in expected conditions; this is a robustness note, not a correctness failure.
- **Task Requirement:** Success: introducing a forced behavior:'smooth' regardless of reduced-motion makes the test fail
