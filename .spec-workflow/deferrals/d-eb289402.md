## Context
Found while writing the /contributions e2e coverage (task 23). Req 3.10 requires that the heatmap SVG not CAUSE horizontal overflow, which is narrower than "the page has none". The e2e spec asserts causation instead: a differential against /resources (a page with no heatmap), no section descendant extending past the viewport, and .contrib-heatmap__scroll never actually scrolling. That guards the heatmap while leaving the underlying bug unmasked. Fixing the footer was out of scope: it is a shared layout component on every route, so it needs verification across all of them rather than being folded into a contributions change.

## Decision Deferred
Not fixed. A link row in src/components/layout/footer.tsx makes document.body 342px wide at a 320px viewport (22px of horizontal overflow) on every (site) route, including /, /projects, /resources and /contributions. Task 23 could not assert its literal success criterion of no horizontal body overflow at 320 because every page already fails it for reasons unrelated to the heatmap.

## Revisit Criteria
document.body.scrollWidth at a 320px viewport should equal 320 on /, /projects, /resources and /contributions. Culprit is a link row in src/components/layout/footer.tsx.
