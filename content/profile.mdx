## About

I'm a platform & infrastructure engineer with a decade in production Kubernetes, most recently on one of the largest software platform infrastructures in the world. In January 2026 I was laid off, and I gave myself six months to make self-employment pay my family's bills. This site is where that's happening, in public.

I have ADHD, diagnosed around 30, and so does my older son. A lot of my writing is for the adult who found out late (often through their kid) that the coping strategies which carried them through school and a career stopped working the day parenthood or a bigger job arrived. I'm one of those adults, and I write to them as a peer.

The rest of me: I trade options most weekdays, make music with keyboards, guitar & a rack of software instruments, sit on the board of my son's [kindergarten society](https://lcdspreschool.ca/index.html), run the tech team at [our church](https://www.gardenschurch.ca), do Pilates, and [read a lot](https://app.thestorygraph.com/profile/madmatt112). I've stopped trying to pick one of these to lead with. It's all me.

## What I do

**Mossfoot Digital** is my consultancy & software firm. On the consulting side: fractional IT leadership, technology & security assessments, migration delivery, and tailored contract work for nonprofits and small businesses in Edmonton and remotely across Canada. On the software side it's the home for the products I build, starting with [Tradr](https://www.tradr.cloud), an open-source trading journal with a free-forever hosted tier and a public [repo](https://github.com/madmatt112/tradr).

**Field Notes** is my newsletter: the story of getting from laid off to sustainably self-employed, told while it happens, plus the tools I use, the systems I build, and the people I meet. Essays live on this site, and the ones tagged field-notes go out by email. [Subscribe here](/newsletter).

**Late-diagnosed ADHD.** I'm building a peer coaching practice for adults who got their diagnosis after 30. It isn't open yet. Field Notes is where I'll say when it is, and where the writing for that reader goes in the meantime. The work behind it is my own healing, drawn from more than one tradition: classic Jungian psychodynamics and psychoanalysis, somatic experiential practice and soul work, guided visualizations and journaling. The aims are to heal past wounds, disrupt generational trauma, and reparent myself.

Day to day I'm also an options daytrader, which keeps the lights on while the rest grows, and is the reason Tradr exists at all.

I'd still take a serious look at the right senior platform or SRE role. That's a footnote on purpose.
