GFM footnotes survive a few transformations on their way from markdown to rendered HTML, and a fixture that pins three live references is enough to catch the common regressions.

The remark-gfm plugin parses `[^N]` references into a footnote-reference node[^1] and emits a definitions block at the end of the document. The order of definitions in source does not have to match reference order; the renderer collects them by identifier.

A second reference in the same paragraph[^2] confirms the parser does not collapse adjacent references. The third reference[^3] lands in its own paragraph so the assertion can verify cross-paragraph linkage works too.

[^1]: First footnote definition. Renders as item 1 in the footnotes section.
[^2]: Second footnote definition. Verifies inline-adjacent references resolve independently.
[^3]: Third footnote definition. Last in source, last in the rendered list.
