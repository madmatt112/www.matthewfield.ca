## Day Job

### Trading

I trade options on the NYSE & Nasdaq using the IBKR brokerage, Monday to Friday, in my home office. I clock in to work as early as I can, but I'm not often ready for the opening bell (7:30am my time). I trade until close (2pm), review the day, and plan for tomorrow.

I use [tradr](https://www.tradr.cloud) to identify trade ideas, journal my trades, and track my performance.

Often the middle of the day is quiet with little market movement - I use that time to work on side-projects and handle other obligations.
