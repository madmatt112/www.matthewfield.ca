## About

I'm an infrastructure and platform engineer with a decade of experience building reliable systems, developer tooling, and the boring glue that keeps production calm. I care about feedback loops, paved roads, and operational ergonomics — the work that makes other engineers faster without making them think about it.

## What I do

Most of my recent work has been in cloud platforms, CI/CD, observability, and the messy seam between application code and the runtime it ships on. I gravitate toward problems where the failure modes are interesting and the fix outlives the incident.

## How I work

Boring code, explicit tradeoffs, short feedback loops. I'd rather ship a small thing that's correct than a large thing that's mostly correct. I write things down so the next person doesn't have to reconstruct the reasoning from a Slack thread.
