## Context
profile-resume tasks v1 specified a scanner and its test but none of the surrounding machinery. Adversarial review r1 of the tasks doc correctly called this the worst of three options: full parity (scanner + src/__fixtures__ canary with pinned sentinels + a third verify-*-canary-regex-pair.mjs + a ci.yml step, modelled on check-content-chokepoint.ts) is roughly 700 lines, while half-parity enforces nothing at all. Cut rather than half-built because no acceptance criterion in profile-resume requires it, and the risk it mitigates is low here: there is exactly one selector module for all three symbols. Note the convention is NOT inert — verify-canary-regex-pair.mjs and verify-content-canary-regex-pair.mjs both run in CI (ci.yml:50-54) — so this is a deliberate scope decision, not a judgement that the pattern is worthless.

## Decision Deferred
Not implemented: a chokepoint scanner asserting the `experience`, `skills`, and `education` symbols from `#site/content` are imported only by their selector module — and the canary fixture, paired-merge verifier, and CI step that give the existing scanners their teeth.

## Revisit Criteria
More than one module imports the collections, or a visibility filter exists in the selector that a direct import would bypass.
